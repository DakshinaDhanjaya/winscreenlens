# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **WinScreenLens** project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or location.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming, inclusive, and constructive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users of the software.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback in Issues and Pull Requests.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening a private contact or issue with the repository maintainer (`DakshinaDhanjaya`).
